# CareFlow Product Context

CareFlow is a flagship portfolio demonstration of a role-based hospital operations workstation. It helps reception, nursing, doctor, pharmacy, cashier, and admin staff understand and advance fictional outpatient visits through registration, triage, consultation, pharmacy, billing, discharge, and audit history.

## Users and environment

The primary scene is a shared clinical workstation under bright overhead lighting during an active shift. Users scan information quickly, may be interrupted, and need deterministic state, visible priority, and clear handoffs more than decorative visual novelty.

## Product principles

- Make the next operational handoff obvious.
- Keep clinical judgment with staff; never suggest a diagnosis.
- Pair every status color with text or shape.
- Keep public queue identifiers separate from patient details.
- Use fictional records and clearly label the demo environment.
- Favor high information density with strong grouping over generic card grids.
- Treat accessibility, keyboard operation, and reduced motion as baseline quality.

## Quality bar

Portfolio flagship: the implementation should feel credible in a real hospital operations setting, remain visually distinctive in screenshots, and preserve every demonstrated workflow without claiming production healthcare readiness.
